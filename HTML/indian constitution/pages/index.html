About the Indian Constitution

The Constitution of India is the supreme law of the country. It was adopted on 26 November 1949 and came into effect on 26 January 1950.

Dr. B. R. Ambedkar is known as the Father of the Indian Constitution.

Important Features

  • Written Constitution
  • Longest Constitution in the World
  • Parliamentary System
  • Federal Structure
  • Independent Judiciary
  • Secular State

Fundamental Rights

  1. Right to Equality
  2. Right to Freedom
  3. Right against Exploitation
  4. Right to Freedom of Religion
  5. Cultural and Educational Rights
  6. Right to Constitutional Remedies

Fundamental Duties

  • Respect the National Flag and National Anthem.
  • Protect the environment.
  • Promote harmony among all citizens.
  • Safeguard public property.
  • Develop scientific thinking.

Did You Know?

The original Constitution was handwritten in both English and Hindi.

About The Workshop

Fabrication Done The Way It Should Be

Every body that leaves VM Engineering Works is welded, measured and fitted on-site in Krishnagiri — not mass-produced. That means the load bed, the container, or the trailer frame is built to match your exact chassis and your exact load, not a generic template.

  • 01 Steel-frame construction fitted to your vehicle's exact chassis
  • 02 Reinforced stake-body and container builds for daily commercial use
  • 03 Tractor trailers and water tanks built for farm and transport work
  • 04 Straightforward pricing, honest timelines, work you can inspect

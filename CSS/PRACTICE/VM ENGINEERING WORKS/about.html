About VM Engineering Works

Our Story

A Workshop Built On Real Fabrication Work

VM Engineering Works operates out of Krishnagiri, Tamil Nadu, fabricating vehicle bodies for mini trucks, pickups, tractors and commercial carriers. Every project starts with the chassis in front of us — measured, framed and welded to match, not pulled off a standard template. That approach is why owners come back when their next vehicle needs a body.

  • 01 Hands-on fabrication — every body measured and welded on-site
  • 02 Work across mini trucks, pickups, tractors and commercial carriers
  • 03 Direct communication with the workshop, no middlemen
  • 04 Fair, transparent pricing agreed before work begins
